Our Plumbing Services

West Bloomfield Plumbing is your one-stop shop for all plumbing needs — residential and commercial. Call if you don’t see what you’re looking for.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drain repair, clogged toilet repair, floor drain back-up, and grease trap repair. We use advanced equipment that is both safe and effective. Basement drain backups diagnosed with camera inspection technology to find the exact source of the problem.

Pipe Leak Repair & Replacement

A pipe leak or cracked pipe can cause major damage quickly. Shut off the water first, then call us. We repair or replace all types of pipes — water lines, sewer lines, water mains, and frozen or burst pipes. The plumber who comes out can also assist with your insurance claim.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

We service all makes and models of water heaters. Most repairs are completed in a single visit. New water heater installations come with a warranty from both the manufacturer and from us.

Toilet Repair & Replacement

Broken toilet repair and replacement, clogged toilet repair, running toilet. We install new toilets from American Standard, Kohler, Moen, and other major brands. Fast, clean service with an upfront estimate.

Faucet, Fixture & Disposal

Leaky faucet repair and replacement, shower and tub faucet repair, garbage disposal repair and installation, instant hot installation, new appliance installation, refrigerator water lines, and more.

Emergency Plumbing

We offer true 24-hour emergency plumbing service every day of the year. Call us day or night for burst pipes, major leaks, clogged drains, or any plumbing emergency. We have a plumber standing by to get to you fast.

Sewer Line & Water Main

Sewer line installation, water main repairs, and leaking valve repair and replacement. We use camera inspection technology to locate and diagnose sewer problems quickly and accurately.

Plumbing Installations

We install sinks, toilets, showers, tubs, water heaters, garbage disposals, instant-hot units, and much more. We have great relationships with Kohler, American Standard, Delta, and Moen and pass those savings on to you.

Commercial Plumbing

We are experts in all aspects of residential and commercial plumbing projects. Offices, retail spaces, restaurants, warehouses, and more — over 20 years of experience means we can handle virtually any commercial plumbing project.
