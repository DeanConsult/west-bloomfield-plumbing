Our Story

West Bloomfield’s Most Trusted Plumbing Company

West Bloomfield Plumbing has over 20 years of experience in almost all plumbing applications. We have an amazing reputation in the West Bloomfield MI area and we service most of Metro Detroit. We offer 24-hour emergency service and have special evening and weekend hours for your convenience.

Most of the calls that come in now are from repeat customers and referrals — which tells us we must be doing something right. Please give us a call and we will be happy to help you with any of your plumbing needs. Our plumbers are well trained, ready to help, and always provide an estimate before any work begins.

On June 4, 2021, West Bloomfield Plumbing received notice from Metro Detroit Review, an online newspaper, that we made their top 25 plumbers in the Metro Detroit Area. We are so proud of this and want to acknowledge all of our customers and friends in West Bloomfield MI. We also became a proud sponsor of Plumbing News and More in November 2021.
