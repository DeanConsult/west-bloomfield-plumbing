What We Do

Expert Plumbing Services

We handle all types of residential and commercial plumbing repairs and installations in West Bloomfield MI and the surrounding Metro Detroit area.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sinks, and toilets cleared fast. We use the latest technology to safely and effectively remove blockages and keep your pipes protected.

Pipe Leak Repair

A pipe leak needs fast attention. Shut off the water, then call us. We repair or replace cracked pipes, burst pipes, and frozen pipes — any time of day.

Water Heater Repair

We service all makes and models of water heaters. Most repairs are done in one visit. New installations come with a full warranty from us and the manufacturer.

Faucet & Disposal

Leaky faucet repair, shower and tub repair, garbage disposal repair and installation, instant hot units, and refrigerator water lines. All major brands.

Plumbing Installations

New sinks, toilets, showers, tubs, water heaters, garbage disposals, and more. We work with Kohler, American Standard, Delta, and Moen at special pricing.

Emergency Plumbing

24-hour emergency plumbing service every day of the year. Call us for burst pipes, major leaks, or any urgent plumbing situation. We will get to you fast.
